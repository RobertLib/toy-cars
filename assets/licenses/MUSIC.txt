(MintoDog's music)" collection on OpenGameArt:
https://opengameart.org/content/for-racing-game-mintodogs-music

Every file is dedicated to the public domain under Creative Commons Zero 1.0
(CC0 1.0): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Attribution is not required; it is given here with thanks. The files are the
published Ogg Vorbis releases, unmodified apart from their names.
