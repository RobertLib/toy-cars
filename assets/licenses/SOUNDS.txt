ToyCars sound effects
=====================

Every recording in assets/sounds is dedicated to the public domain under
Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0 1.0):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Attribution is not required; it is given here with thanks.

The files are edited from the sources below: trimmed, mixed to mono (ambience
beds stay stereo), filtered, looped, layered where noted, normalized and
encoded as Ogg Vorbis. tools/build_sounds.py lists every edit and rebuilds them.
Freesound sources are its public high-quality previews.
